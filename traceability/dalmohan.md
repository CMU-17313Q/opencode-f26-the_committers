# Traceability: Mistake classification for /mistakes (dalmohan)

Target feature: mistake classification, which decides when two recorded mistakes are "the same kind" for the Mistake Pattern Recognition feature (issues 2 and 3).

- Classifier: [student-error-classifier.ts](../packages/core/src/session/student-error-classifier.ts)
- Summary that uses it: [student-error-summary.ts](../packages/core/src/session/student-error-summary.ts)
- Mistake record it compares: [student-error.ts](../packages/core/src/session/student-error.ts)

Property tests: [student-error-classifier.property.test.ts](../packages/opencode/test/student-error/student-error-classifier.property.test.ts). They live in `packages/opencode` because that is where the team added fast-check (PR #33), and they import the classifier, summary, and parser from `@opencode-ai/core`. Run them with `cd packages/opencode && bun test test/student-error/student-error-classifier.property.test.ts`. CI runs them in their own step of the `unit` job in `test.yml`, so the log lists each property.

Evidence IDs come from my P4A workbook: C- lines are the classmate interview, S- lines the course staff interview, and O- lines the outside interview.

## Generators

- **Mistake catalog**: real TypeScript compiler messages and failed-test names, because those are the only mistakes the parser records. Each entry is one error type in the interviewees' sense (S-8, C-8): undefined name, missing property, type mismatch, argument type, argument count, not callable, missing token, expression expected, missing module, and two failed tests. Undefined name and missing property each have a "Did you mean ...?" variant, which is how the bug was found. Different types often share words ("expected", "assignable", "Cannot find") and names, which is the case interviewees worried about (C-4, S-4, O-4).
- **Names**: short and long, camelCase, starting with `_` or `$`, digits after the first letter, and names that are also words in the messages ("name", "type", "find").
- **Files and lines**: different folders and extensions, lines from 1 to 5000 (C-8, O-8).
- **Capitalization**: all upper, all lower, and mixed case (C-8, O-8).
- **Histories**: 1 to 12 mistakes from a few kinds, so repeats are common. R5, R6, and R7 use at most 3 kinds, because the summary lists at most 3 recurring kinds; with more, a kind could be hidden by the cut, which is the summary's design, not a counting error.
- **Clean runs**: output with no errors, like after a fix (C-6, S-6): "Found 0 errors.", passing tests, " 12 pass", " 0 fail". The R5 and R6 properties check that the parser records nothing for these.
